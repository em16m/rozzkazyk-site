Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how autovideo, the internal publishing tool of the ROZZKAZYK channel ("we"), handles data when it connects to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It is not available to the public and does not collect data from viewers.

What data we access

  • Basic account information of the connected accounts (account ID, name), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and edit the channel's own videos and their titles, descriptions and privacy settings.
  • Aggregated performance statistics of the channel's own videos (views, watch time), to choose publishing times.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. Data is used solely to publish and analyze the channel's own content.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time: Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok and Instagram — in the app's security / connected apps settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.
